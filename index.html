Wachusett GPS pass questions

Quick answers about the group rate and checkout process.

What is a Wachusett GPS Pass?

GPS stands for Greatest Possible Savings. It is Wachusett Mountain's group season pass program. An eligible GPS Gold, Silver, or Bronze pass provides the same mountain access as the corresponding regular pass, at the GPS group price shown by Wachusett.

How do I use the SKICLUB GPS code?

Choose an eligible pass on Wachusett's official group season pass page, add it to your cart, and enter SKICLUB in the GPS Club or group name field before completing checkout.

Do I need to register with SKICLUB GPS?

No separate registration is required on this website. Wachusett handles the purchase, payment, pass terms, and fulfillment directly.

How current are the prices?

The regular and GPS prices are synchronized from Wachusett's official season pass pages several times each day. Wachusett remains the source of truth for final pricing, eligibility, restrictions, and availability.
